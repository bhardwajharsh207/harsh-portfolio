## Problem

The first version of the instructor co-pilot had the right ambition but the wrong shape. It packed too much information into a moment where instructors needed fast, focused guidance.

The result was weak adoption.

## My Role

I owned the recovery path: understand why V1 was failing, partner with design and research, make hard scope decisions, and relaunch a simpler product experience.

## Product Approach

The key insight was that the instructor did not need a large analytics surface during a counseling conversation. They needed a fast way to access essential recommendations and talking points.

The redesign moved toward a focused single-screen workflow with simpler navigation and a tighter information hierarchy.

## Technical Context

This work depended on more than interface cleanup. It required understanding what data was available, what recommendations were actually useful in the counseling moment, and what backend/data work was needed to support the streamlined experience.

## Tradeoff

The hardest decision was cutting features the team had already spent time building. The benefit was a product that fit the real workflow better.

## Outcome

The public-safe outcome is that the redesign turned a weak V1 launch into a more usable and adopted instructor workflow.
