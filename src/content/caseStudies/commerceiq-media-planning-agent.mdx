## Problem

Customers saw value in day-to-day automation, but they wanted more help with strategic media planning. The issue was not simply that the product needed another dashboard. It needed to help customers decide what to do.

## My Role

I analyzed customer feedback, identified the strategic planning gaps, worked with internal experts, and helped move the strongest recommendations from pilot to product workflow.

## Product Approach

The team first validated the work through a high-touch pilot. That made the product work more grounded: instead of guessing what a planning product should do, we could see which recommendations customers actually accepted and found useful.

## Technical Context

The planning workflow touched allocation, marketplace/channel context, simulation, explainability, and keyword recommendations. The technical product challenge was making recommendations understandable enough for customers to trust.

## Tradeoff

The first pilot was not fully scalable, but it reduced product risk. It helped separate what required expert judgment from what could become repeatable product functionality.

## Outcome

The work improved the strategic value of the product, created stronger customer confidence, and gave the team a clearer path to productize media planning.
