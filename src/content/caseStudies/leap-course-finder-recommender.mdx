## Problem

Instructors were spending too much time creating personalized college and course recommendations. The work depended on manual web searches, spreadsheets, and repeated filtering against each student's profile.

## My Role

I owned the product direction for simplifying the instructor workflow: understand the recommendation process, define the searchable data layer, and design a tool that let instructors move faster without reducing quality.

## Product Approach

The core decision was to convert repeated manual lookup into a structured search and recommendation workflow. The product preserved instructor judgment while removing the slowest operational work.

## Technical Context

The system needed usable education data, verification, search filters, and recommendation logic across student preferences such as budget, location, course category, academic background, and test profile.

## Tradeoff

The interface needed to feel simple, but the filtering logic underneath was complex. I chose a familiar search experience so instructors could adopt the tool quickly while the system handled the heavier matching logic.

## Outcome

The public-safe outcome is that the tool compressed a manual recommendation workflow into a more scalable product process and increased instructor leverage.
