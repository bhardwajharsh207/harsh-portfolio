## Problem

The product had a classic adoption puzzle: the headline metric was low, but the underlying reasons were different across customer groups. Some customers had never really tried the feature. Others had tried it and stopped.

Those two groups needed different product responses.

## My Role

I pulled usage data, split customers into behavior-based segments, worked with Customer Success to understand the "why," and translated the findings into a roadmap across onboarding, automation, UX, ML trust, and marketplace coverage.

## Product Approach

The work began with segmentation. New or unaware users needed better enablement, help content, office hours, and first-time guidance. Lapsed users needed deeper product fixes: automation, smoother UX, more trust in recommendations, and better marketplace fit.

## Technical Context

The product involved ML-supported optimization. That meant adoption was partly about model trust, not just usability. Users needed to understand why the system recommended an action and how that action fit into their daily campaign workflow.

## Tradeoff

The team could not fix every blocker at once. I prioritized the adoption roadmap so each cycle had a clear problem to solve and a measurable behavior to watch.

## Outcome

Regular product usage moved in the right direction, customer education improved, the workflow became easier to trust, and marketplace coverage expanded.
