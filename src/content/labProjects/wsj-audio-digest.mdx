## Experiment

The first version avoids fragile login automation. It starts with a proven manual source path, then automates extraction, sectioning, audio synthesis, and morning delivery.

## Why It Belongs Here

This shows practical automation judgment: build the reliable local loop first, avoid legal or operational overreach, and only automate the access path after it is validated.
