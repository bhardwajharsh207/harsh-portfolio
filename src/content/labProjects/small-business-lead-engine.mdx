## Experiment

The first version is intentionally service-led. The goal is to learn whether small businesses will pay for a concrete local revenue system before overbuilding SaaS.

## Why It Belongs Here

This shows product thinking outside a formal job: customer discovery, MVP scoping, distribution, service design, and the discipline to test demand before building too much software.
