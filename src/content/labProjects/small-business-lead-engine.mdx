## Experiment

The first version is intentionally service-led. The goal is to learn whether small businesses will pay for a concrete local revenue system before overbuilding SaaS.

## Product Website Direction

The product page should feel like a practical local-business offer, not a software landing page trying too hard. The first version should explain the outcome plainly: more qualified local leads, cleaner follow-up, and less manual chaos for the owner.

Core sections to build later:

- Who it is for: local service businesses with weak lead capture or follow-up
- What gets set up: web presence, Google Business Profile, reviews, CRM, and follow-up
- Proof: before/after local presence audit and first customer result
- CTA: request a local growth audit

## Why It Belongs Here

This shows product thinking outside a formal job: customer discovery, MVP scoping, distribution, service design, and the discipline to test demand before building too much software.
