## Why Build The Portfolio This Way

The portfolio is not just a set of pages. It is a product system for proof of work.

The goal is to make it easy to add case studies, lab projects, writing, artifacts, and future product experiments without redesigning the site every time.

## Product Choices

- Astro keeps the site fast and simple.
- MDX keeps content editable.
- Content collections make case studies and lab projects structured.
- A private source layer keeps raw notes separate from public writing.

## What Comes Next

The next milestone is adding stronger artifacts: diagrams, public demos, screenshots, and build logs tied to real product experiments.
