## Why These Two Experiments

I am using the lab section to show how I think when the product is not yet fully built. The point is not to pretend these are polished companies. The point is to show the early product work: user, problem, wedge, MVP, risk, and first proof.

The first experiment is a small business lead engine: testing whether local businesses will pay for a done-for-you lead-generation setup before building full SaaS.

The second experiment is a WSJ audio digest: testing whether a local-first reading-to-audio pipeline can make the print-edition workflow easier to consume.

## Current Stage

Both are in idea/MVP definition. The next proof should be evidence from the first manual loop, not a polished demo without users.

## What I Want To Prove

- Can the lead engine get one real business owner to care enough to try a manual version?
- Can the audio digest preserve the structure of a print edition while becoming easier to consume?
- Can both ideas become small but real products without overbuilding the first version?
